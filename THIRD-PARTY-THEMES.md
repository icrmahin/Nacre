# Third-party palette credits

The seven additional color themes are independent Nacre adaptations. They reuse Nacre's existing TextMate and semantic scope coverage and apply palette colors inspired by the projects below. They do not bundle the upstream extensions' implementation code, artwork, icons or fonts.
